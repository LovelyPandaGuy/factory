# Founding Builders

The first 100 engineers to use Next-Gen Factory on their real work, every day, wherever they
are on the continent. What they build and the lessons they share are the foundation the
movement stands on. They will be remembered as the people who proved Africa could build for
the world.
[What this means](MOVEMENT.md#the-first-100-founding-builders).

## How to add your name

Open a pull request that adds one row to the table below, in the order people joined. Use
the name you want shown, your GitHub handle, and the date you started using the factory.

## Lessons by Founding Builders

Every lesson carries its author's name in [`lessons/`](lessons/). The best ones become
