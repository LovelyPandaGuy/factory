The shift

Code is becoming cheap. Trust is becoming everything.

When something becomes cheap, the value moves to what is still scarce. AI made building fast and nearly free. What it did not make free is knowing that what was built is right, and proving it.

Before AI

Building was the hard part

Software was slow and expensive to write. The money went to whoever could write the code.

Now

Anyone can build, fast

AI writes a growing share of the world's code. It looks finished. Often it is not, and nobody can tell which is which.

What becomes valuable

Proof that it works

The scarce thing is certainty. Whoever can prove AI-built work is right sits at the centre of the AI economy. Nobody owns that yet.

How it works

Every build leaves proof.

Next-Gen Factory works with the AI coding tool you already use: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or OpenCode. Your AI does the work. The factory makes sure it is the right work, and that it works.

  1. It plans from your codeWhat will change, the situations people forget, and how each part will be proven.
  2. You approveYou read only what matters, and press yes.
  3. Your AI builds itThe fast part, now open to everyone.
  4. The factory checks itYour tests and build run. Failures go back to the builder.
  5. It uses the app like a personIn a real browser, with a screenshot for every check.
  6. A second AI reviews itA separate session that never saw how the first one thought.
  7. You get the proofAnd you decide whether to keep the change.
