# Next-Gen Collective brand

The mark, the colours and the voice used in this repository. Everything outward-facing
follows this file. Values come from the organisation's visual system, not from taste.

## The mark

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `assets/nextgen-logo-light.png` | Light backgrounds (GitHub light theme, documents, print) |
| `assets/nextgen-logo-dark.png` | Dark backgrounds (GitHub dark theme, covers, dark panels) |

Use the supplied files. Do not recolour, stretch, crop or rebuild the mark. Keep the gold
frame and the spacing around it. Do not put it on a busy photo; use a plain ground.

The name is written **Next-Gen Collective**, always hyphenated. The tagline is
**Innovate. Elevate. Inspire the future.**

The logo and name are the organisation's marks. The MIT licence covers the code in this
repository; it does not grant the right to use the name or the logo.

## Colour

Gold is an accent, never a background. The canvas is light-first: cream or white, with
near-black ink. Dark grounds are for covers and section breaks.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0e0e0f` | Headings, dark panels, numbers |
| Cover | `#0b0b0c` | Covers and closing grounds |
| Cream | `#fbf8f1` | Page background |
| Sand | `#f3ede0` | Quiet panels and boxes |
| Card | `#ffffff` | Cards, with a `#e4dccb` hairline |
| Text | `#2b2925` | Body copy |
| Muted | `#5e5a52` | Captions, footers, secondary copy |
| Gold | `#f5be40` | Accents, emphasis, figures, rules |
| Gold deep | `#a87a10` | Small labels and numerals on light backgrounds |
| Red | `#b4412f` | Risk only. Never decoration |
| On dark | `#e7e2d8` | Body copy on ink |

Do not introduce another accent. Do not use red for anything except a real risk.

## Type

Both faces are built into macOS; render documents on a Mac. Do not bundle or redistribute
the fonts.

| Role | Face | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Cover and section titles | Baskerville (final phrase in Baskerville Italic) | 37pt cover, 25pt section |
| Card titles | Baskerville | 11–13pt |
| Figures | Baskerville | 30pt, gold on ink |
| Eyebrow labels | Avenir Next Demi Bold, uppercase, `.24em` tracking | 7.6pt, gold deep |
| Body | Avenir Next | 10.6pt |
| Footer | Avenir Next | 7pt muted |

Fallbacks: Libre Baskerville and Nunito Sans. They are close, not exact.

## Voice

Formal, evidence-led, no hype. The audience is people who fund, approve or depend on the
work.

- Numbers, not adjectives: "45 of 47 problems fixed", not "a huge improvement".
- Short declarative lines.
- Ambitious about Africa without being sentimental about it.
- British and South African spelling: organisation, programme, recognise.

In this repository that means: say what passed, what was flagged and what was not checked.
Never say "bug-free", "secure" or "compliant".

## How this repository uses it

- The README shows the mark, choosing the light or dark file with the reader's theme.
- Documents and reports use cream, ink and gold; red only for a real risk.
- The terminal keeps colour for meaning (pass, fail, not checked); it does not use the
  brand palette to decorate output.
